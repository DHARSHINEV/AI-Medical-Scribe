Why MediScribe? INNOVATION
"Clinical Safety Second Look"

Commercial scribes simply transcribe. MediScribe acts as a safety second look:

  • Cross-Checks EHR History: Catches allergy and medication conflicts.
  • Detects Gaps: Flags missing dosage, frequency, or durations.
  • Explainable Evidence: Links every SOAP line to raw speech.
  • Lab Range Comparison: Identifies out-of-range pathology tests.
